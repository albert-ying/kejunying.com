OpenScientist is an open-source agentic system that investigates scientist-defined biomedical questions through data analysis, information retrieval and hypothesis generation. Expert evaluation across four clinical case studies assessed its predictions and hypotheses, including validation in an external cohort and comparison with randomized controls.
