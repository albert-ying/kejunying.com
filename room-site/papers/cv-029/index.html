Integration of proteomic aging clocks in a phase 2a clinical trial supports simultaneous geroprotective assessment

DOI 10.1038/s41587-026-03286-y

Zhavoronkov, A., Galkin, F., Chen, S., ..., Ying, K., ..., Gladyshev, V. N.

Nature Biotechnology · 2026

Open paper

Summary

All six proteomic clocks predicted lower biological age in treated arms of a 12-week phase 2a rentosertib trial in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis. The clocks could not separate aging effects from disease effects, although pathway analyses suggested changes in senescence and metabolism alongside anti-fibrotic activity.
