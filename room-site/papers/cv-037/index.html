ECloudGen: leveraging electron clouds as a latent variable to scale up structure-based molecular design

DOI 10.1038/s43588-025-00886-7

Zhang, O., ..., Ying, K., Huang, Y., Zhao, H., Kang, Y., Pan, P., Wang, J., Guo, D., Zheng, S., Hsieh, C.-Y., & Hou, T.

Nature Computational Science · 2025

Open paper

Summary

ECloudGen uses electron clouds as latent representations to connect ligand-only data with protein–ligand complex data for structure-based molecule generation. In the reported benchmarks, it generated binders with better potency and physicochemical properties and covered a broader chemical space than the comparison methods.
