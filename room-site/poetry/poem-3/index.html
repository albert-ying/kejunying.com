浣溪沙·皂衫薄

绛帐无风日影灼 佳人慵起朱颜酡。
樱唇闲启指轻啄。
欲叹春残花渐老 却闻夏盛皂衫薄。
玉肌香透软烟罗。

墨尘子 于广州 己亥年 四月 十四

In the poem "Huan Xi Sha" (浣溪沙), the author describes a sensual and intimate scene during the transition from spring to summer. Inside the crimson curtains ("绛帐"), there is no wind, and the sun's shadow is scorching ("日影灼"). The beautiful person ("佳人") is slow to rise, their rosy cheeks flushed ("朱颜酡"). Their cherry lips ("樱唇") part leisurely, and their fingers lightly tap ("指轻啄"), hinting at playful and intimate gestures. The poet sighs, lamenting that spring is fading and the flowers are gradually aging ("欲叹春残花渐老"). However, they then note that summer is in full bloom, and the dark shirt ("皂衫") is thin ("薄"), suggesting the lightness and transparency of the garment in the summer heat. The lover's jade-like skin ("玉肌") emits a fragrance that permeates the soft, gauzy fabric ("软烟罗").
