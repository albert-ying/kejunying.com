A world model simulates the latent dynamics of human health

DOI 10.64898/2026.09.19.26363460

Wang, Z., Zhang, O., Wang, J., Jiang, Y., Ma, Z. C., …, Gladyshev, V. N., Wyss-Coray, T., Ying, K.

medRxiv · 2026 · Preprint

Open paper

Summary

HealthFlux is a world model built from 502,166 UK Biobank participants. It treats a person's health as one hidden state that changes between visits and is updated by each new blood test, diagnosis, molecular profile or brain and body MRI. In held-out participants it predicts 1,256 diseases and death over twenty years, and it was tested in four independent US cohorts.

  • HealthFlux learns the latent dynamics of health from asynchronous pan-modal records.
  • Disease prediction from a persistent and revisable health state.
  • HealthFlux enables transfer across diseases, measurements and populations.
  • HealthFlux simulates medication effects on physiology and disease risk.
  • Pan-modal organization of the shared health state.
  • HealthFlux relates structural imaging to disease risk.
