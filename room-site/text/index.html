Hello, I'm Kejun Albert Ying (应可钧), an incoming assistant professor. My lab opens in the Bay Area in January 2027.

I'm a postdoctoral fellow with Tony Wyss-Coray at Stanford University and David Baker at the University of Washington, and I earned my Ph.D. at Harvard Medical School with Vadim Gladyshev.

I build AI for biology and aging: foundation models and world models of biological data, and agents that discover aging interventions.

Our latest work is HealthFlux, a world model of human health that predicts 1,256 diseases and death over twenty years.

Solve aging first, and with time, we'll solve all remaining challenges of humanity.
