PRC2-AgeIndex as a universal biomarker of aging and rejuvenation

DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-50098-2

Moqri, M., ..., de Sena Brandine, G., Ying, K., Tarkhov, A., ..., Sebastiano, V.

Nature Communications · 2024

Open paper

Summary

Regions strongly bound by PRC2 in embryonic stem cells gained DNA methylation with age across the examined somatic mitotic cells. The authors propose their average methylation, the PRC2-AgeIndex, as a cellular aging biomarker that distinguishes responses to aging interventions.
