Acarbose Suppresses Symptoms of Mitochondrial Disease in a Mouse Model of Leigh Syndrome

DOI 10.1038/s42255-023-00815-w

A Bitto, AS Grillo, TK Ito, IB Stanaway, BMG Nguyen, K Ying, …, M Kaeberlein

Nature Metabolism · 2023

Open paper

Summary

Acarbose reduced disease symptoms and improved survival in a mouse model of Leigh syndrome independently of mTOR inhibition. Its effects added to those of rapamycin, and the study suggested that changes in the microbiome may contribute.
