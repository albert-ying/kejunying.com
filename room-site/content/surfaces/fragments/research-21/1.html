bioRxiv · 2022 · Preprint

Open paper

This preprint compared tissue and single-cell methylation during development and aging and found that epigenetic aging includes coordinated changes but is dominated by a stochastic component. It also described an algorithm for identifying coordinated and stochastic CpG clusters with corresponding transcriptional patterns.
