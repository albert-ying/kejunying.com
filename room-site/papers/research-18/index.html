Biolearn, an Open-Source Library for Biomarkers of Aging

DOI 10.1101/2023.12.02.569722

K Ying, S Paulson, M Perez-Guevara, M Emamifar, M Casas Martinez, D Kwon, JR Poganik, M Moqri, VN Gladyshev

bioRxiv · 2023 · Preprint

Open paper

Summary

By furnishing a community-driven platform, Biolearn significantly augments the development, assessment, and validation trajectories of aging biomarkers, paving the way toward more rigorous clinical validation and, ultimately, application in clinical trials targeting healthy longevity.
