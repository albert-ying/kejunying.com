Circular RNAs as regulators and biomarkers of mammalian ovarian ageing

DOI 10.1007/s11357-025-01798-0

Grzeczka, A., Iqbal, S., Ying, K., Kordowitzki, P.

GeroScience · 2025

Open paper

Summary

This review examines circular RNAs in ovarian development and aging, including their regulation of proteins, other RNAs and gene activity. It considers their potential as biomarkers of mammalian ovarian aging.
