We use biobank cohorts and human genetics to ask what shapes how long people live and stay healthy. Epigenome-wide Mendelian randomization identified DNA-methylation sites that are potentially causal for aging-related traits, and whole-exome sequencing showed that centenarians carry an 11 to 22% lower burden of rare loss-of-function mutations than controls. Two open resources let the field compare biomarkers on the same data. Biolearn evaluates 39 aging biomarkers on standardized data from more than 20,000 people, and the Biomarkers of Aging Challenge ran an open competition on blinded methylation and proteomic data from 500 Mass General Brigham Biobank participants.
