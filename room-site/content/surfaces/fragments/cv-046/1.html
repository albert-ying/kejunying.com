Nature Aging · 2024

Open paper

The study links spontaneous B cell lymphoma in aged mice to the expansion of age-associated clonal B cells with increased biological age. It describes both cell-intrinsic and microenvironmental changes during progression and reports that mTOR or c-Myc inhibition attenuated pre-malignant changes.
