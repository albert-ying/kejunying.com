ODesign: A World Model for Biomolecular Interaction Design

Zhang, O., Zhang, X., Lin, H., Tan, C., Wang, Q., Mo, Y., ..., Ying, K., Li, J., Zeng, Y., Lang, L., Pan, P., Cao, H., Song, Z., Qiang, B., Wang, J., Ji, P., Bai, L., Zhang, J., Hsieh, C.-Y., Heng, P. A., Sun, S., Hou, T., & Zheng, S.

arXiv · 2025 · Preprint

Open paper

Summary

ODesign is an all-atom generative model for designing interactions between different biomolecular types. It aims to give finer control over interaction details than models specialized for a single molecular type.
