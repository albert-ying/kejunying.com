Precious3GPT: Multimodal Multi-Species Multi-Omics Multi-Tissue Transformer for Aging Research and Drug Discovery

DOI 10.1101/2024.07.25.605062

Galkin, F., ..., Tyshkovskiy, A., Ying, K., Gladyshev, V. N., & Zhavoronkov, A.

bioRxiv · 2024 · Preprint

Open paper

Summary

Precious3GPT combines textual, tabular and knowledge graph representations of biological experiments across species, tissues and omics modalities. The model supports tasks including age prediction, sample classification and prediction of molecular responses to compounds.
