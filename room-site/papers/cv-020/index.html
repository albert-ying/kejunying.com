Biological Age, Aging Clocks, and the Interplay with Lymphoid Neoplasms: Mechanisms and Clinical Frontiers

DOI 10.3390/lymphatics3030019

Wu, X., Liu, H., Ying, K.

Lymphatics · 2025

Open paper

Summary

This review examines links between biological aging, immune aging and lymphoid neoplasms, including associations of aging-clock measures with cancer risk, progression and treatment tolerance. It discusses how biological age assessments might inform clinical care and what remains to be established.
