Total Genetic Contribution Assessment across the Human Genome

DOI 10.1038/s41467-021-23124-w

T Li, Z Ning, Z Yang, R Zhai, C Zheng, W Xu, Y Wang, K Ying, Y Chen, X Shen

Nature Communications · 2021

Open paper

Summary

The study developed total genetic contribution assessment to measure the overall contribution of a gene or variant across human traits without thresholding association signals. Analyses across five UK Biobank phenotype domains identified domain-specific loci and tissue associations.
