## About

<img src="/about/avatar.png" class="sidenote" style="min-width: initial; margin-top: 0rem;" alt="Kejun Albert Ying">

Kejun Ying is an incoming assistant professor. He will start his own lab in the Bay Area in January 2027; the exact institution will be announced later. He is currently a postdoctoral fellow at [Stanford University](https://med.stanford.edu/) in [Tony Wyss-Coray's lab](https://wyss-coray.stanford.edu/) and at the [University of Washington](https://www.washington.edu/) in [David Baker's lab](https://www.bakerlab.org/), supported by an NIH/NIA F99K00 fellowship. He received the 2026 WAIC Yunfan Award.

His research focuses on **AI for Science**, with an emphasis on **AI for Biology** and **AI for Aging**. He develops foundation models and world models for biological data, and builds agentic systems. His work includes biological age modeling and aging intervention discovery, brain aging and neurodegeneration, systematic analyses of cancer and age-related diseases, and de novo protein design for aging interventions. His previous work includes [a causal inference-based aging clock](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-023-00557-0), [ClockBase](https://clockbase.org), and MethylGPT.
