Mammalian aging involves genome-wide splicing degeneration leading to functional decline

DOI 10.64898/2026.06.26.734787

Zhang, S., Tyshkovskiy, A., Ying, K., Wang, S., & Gladyshev, V. N.

bioRxiv · 2026 · Preprint

Open paper

Summary

An analysis of mouse and human transcriptomes found age-associated deterioration in RNA splicing that alters reading frames and protein domains. This splicing degeneration increased with age and was reduced following calorie restriction or rapamycin treatment.
