Novel Exploratory Transcriptomic Candidates as Biomarkers and Cancer Hallmark Fingerprints for Ovarian Endometroid and Clear Cell Carcinomas in Women

DOI 10.3390/antiox15080979

Kordowitzki, P., & Ying, K.

Antioxidants · 2026

Open paper

Summary

An analysis of ovarian cancer gene expression combined differential expression, LASSO regression and Random Forest classification to identify candidate biomarkers distinguishing clear cell from endometrioid carcinomas. The candidates were associated with hypoxia, oxidative stress, DNA repair and metabolic processes; the study was primarily computational.
