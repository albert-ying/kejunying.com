AI for Productivity in the Age of Agentic AI

DOI 10.20944/preprints202608.1248.v1

Xi, Z., Zhou, E., Fang, X., ..., Ying, K., ..., Huang, X.

Preprints.org · 2026 · Preprint

Open paper

Summary

The article distinguishes two ways agentic AI can affect productivity: reducing effort for existing tasks and making previously infeasible work economically viable. It reviews stages of AI system development, adoption across sectors and challenges in reliability, governance and labor markets.
