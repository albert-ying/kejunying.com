Graph neural networks in modern AI-aided drug discovery

DOI 10.1021/acs.chemrev.5c00461

Zhang, O., Lin, H., Zhang, X., Wang, X., Wu, Z., Ye, Q., Zhao, W., Wang, J., Ying, K., Kang, Y., Hsieh, C.-Y., Hou, T.

Chemical Reviews · 2025

Open paper

Summary

This review covers graph neural networks for molecular property prediction, virtual screening, molecule generation, biomedical knowledge graphs and synthesis planning. It examines model design, integration with other learning methods and limitations in drug discovery applications.
