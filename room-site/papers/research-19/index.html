Genetic Landscape of the ACE2 Coronavirus Receptor

DOI 10.1161/circulationaha.121.057888

Z Yang, E Macdonald-Dunlop, J Chen, R Zhai, T Li, A Richmond, …, K Ying, …, X Shen

Circulation · 2022

Open paper

Summary

A genome-wide association meta-analysis of plasma ACE2 levels in more than 28,000 people mapped its genetic basis. The study found a shared genetic basis between circulating ACE2 and cardiovascular, COVID-19 and other related diseases.
