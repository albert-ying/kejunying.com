Characterization of naked mole-rat hematopoiesis reveals unique stem and progenitor cell patterns and neotenic traits

DOI 10.15252/embj.2021109694

Emmrich, S., Trapp, A., Tolibzoda Zakusilo, F., Straight, M. E., Ying, A. K., Tyshkovskiy, A., ..., Gorbunova, V.

The EMBO Journal · 2022

Open paper

Summary

The study mapped naked mole-rat blood and bone marrow cells and found youthful cell compositions and transcriptomes maintained through at least middle age. Their stem and progenitor cells showed prolonged cell cycles, a larger quiescent compartment than in mice and low respiration rates.
