Plasma protein-based organ-specific aging and mortality models unveil diseases as accelerated aging of organismal systems

DOI 10.1016/j.cmet.2024.10.005

Goeminne, L. J. E., Vladimirova, A., Eames, A., Tyshkovskiy, A., Argentieri, M. A., Ying, K., Moqri, M., & Gladyshev, V. N.

Cell Metabolism · 2024

Open paper

Summary

Plasma proteome data from more than 50,000 people were used to build organ-specific models of chronological age and mortality. The models predicted organ-related disease and linked organ aging rates to sex, lifestyle and environmental factors.

published
