MethylGPT is a transformer foundation model trained on 154,063 human DNA methylation profiles from 5,281 datasets across tissues. It covers 49,156 CpG sites and predicts methylation values with a Pearson correlation of 0.93. Its downstream predictions stay stable with up to 70% of the data missing.
