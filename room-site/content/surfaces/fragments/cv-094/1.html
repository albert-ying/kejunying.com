The talk covers two ways to read the aging methylome. Epigenome-wide Mendelian randomization identifies CpG sites potentially causal for aging-related traits and gives DamAge and AdaptAge, clocks for damaging and adaptive changes. MethylGPT, a transformer foundation model trained on 154,063 methylation profiles, learns representations of CpG sites without external supervision.
