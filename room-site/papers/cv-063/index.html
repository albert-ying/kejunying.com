Anatomy of aging through organ-resolved multi-modal imaging and deep learning

DOI 10.64898/2026.03.14.26348392

Eames, A., Glubokov, D., Moldakozhayev, A., Yücel, A. D., Tyshkovskiy, A., Ying, K., Goeminne, L. J. E., de Magalhães, C. G., & Gladyshev, V. N.

medRxiv · 2026 · Preprint

Open paper

Summary

Multimodal imaging and deep learning measured organ-specific aging across 39 anatomical regions in 134,000 UK Biobank participants. The study found heterogeneous aging within and between people, associations with corresponding organ diseases and differing organ responses to lifestyle factors.
