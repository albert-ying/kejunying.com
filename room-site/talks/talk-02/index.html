Decoding the Aging Methylome: From Causal Inference to Foundation Models

2025-10

ASHG 2025 Annual Meeting

Boston, MA

Abstract

In this talk, Kejun Ying will first present how integrating GWAS and Mendelian randomization with methylation data leads to causality-enriched aging clocks that separate detrimental from adaptive changes. He will then introduce MethylGPT, a transformer-based foundation model trained on over 150,000 methylation profiles across diverse populations. The model demonstrates state-of-the-art methylation prediction (R=0.929) and maintains robust performance with up to 70% missing data.
