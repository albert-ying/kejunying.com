DNA methylation profiling in Xenopus laevis embryos placed the minimum epigenetic age near gastrulation, alongside a minimum in methylation entropy and a rapid rise in transcript abundance. This developmental stage also had a higher incidence of disrupted development, suggesting a transition from embryonic rejuvenation to aging.
