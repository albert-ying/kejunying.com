Nature of epigenetic aging from a single-cell perspective

DOI 10.1038/s43587-024-00616-0

Tarkhov, A. E., Lindstrom-Vautrin, T., Zhang, S., Ying, K., Moqri, M., ..., Gladyshev, V. N.

Nature Aging · 2024

Open paper

Summary

Comparing tissue and single-cell DNA methylation in mice showed that epigenetic aging combines coordinated changes with a major stochastic component. The study also developed an algorithm to identify coordinated and stochastic CpG clusters and related them to transcriptional patterns.
