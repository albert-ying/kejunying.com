Nature Reviews Genetics · 2024

Open paper

Summary

This Tools of the Trade article describes epigenome-wide Mendelian randomization, which uses inherited genetic variants to test which DNA methylation sites in an epigenetic clock have potential causal links to lifespan and healthspan.

A perspective on applying causal inference methods to understand the relationship between epigenetic changes and the aging process.
