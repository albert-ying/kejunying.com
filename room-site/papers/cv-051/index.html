Biomarkers of aging for the identification and evaluation of longevity interventions

DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2023.08.003

Moqri, M., Herzog, C., Poganik, J. R., Biomarkers of Aging Consortium, ... Gladyshev, V. N.

Cell · 2023

Open paper

Summary

The article proposes terminology and a classification for biomarkers of aging, with potential clinical uses and validation steps. It identifies the lack of shared standards as a barrier to using these biomarkers to evaluate longevity interventions in people.
