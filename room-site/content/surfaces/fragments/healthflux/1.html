HealthFlux is a world model built from 502,166 UK Biobank participants. It treats a person's health as one hidden state that changes between visits and is updated by each new blood test, diagnosis, molecular profile or brain and body MRI. In held-out participants it predicts 1,256 diseases and death over twenty years, and it was tested in four independent US cohorts.
