The 18S rRNA methyltransferase DIMT-1 regulates lifespan in the germline later in life

DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-62323-7

Rothi, M.H., Sarkar, G.C., Haddad, J.A., Mitchell, W., Ying, K., et al.

Nature Communications · 2025

Open paper

Summary

The study identifies the ribosomal RNA methyltransferase DIMT-1 as a regulator of lifespan and stress resistance in C. elegans. Its effects arise in the germline after mid-life and involve selective translation and signaling from the germline to the rest of the animal.
