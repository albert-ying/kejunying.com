Transplanting white adipose tissue between young and old mice shifted the grafts' molecular age toward the age of the host, while transplantation itself accelerated tissue aging. Old tissue in young hosts showed lower predicted age, changes in thermogenic pathways and altered tissue architecture, indicating partial reversibility of adipose tissue aging.
