In paired CSF and plasma samples from 2,171 older adults, CSF-to-plasma ratios rose with age for 848 proteins and fell for 64. This suggests that the brain barriers regulate proteins selectively. Higher ratios of some proteins made outside the brain were associated with preserved cognition, and genetic loci were linked to the ratios of 241 proteins.
