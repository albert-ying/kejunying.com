Challenges and recommendations for the translation of biomarkers of aging

DOI 10.1038/s43587-024-00683-3

Biomarkers of Aging Consortium, Herzog, C. M. S., Goeminne, L. J. E., Poganik, J. R., ..., Gladyshev, V. N.

Nature Aging · 2024

Open paper

Summary

An expert survey identified six barriers to the clinical translation of biomarkers of aging. The resulting recommendations call for clinically actionable measures, wider affordability and access, and validation of responses in individuals.
