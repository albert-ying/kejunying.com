Disagreement on foundational principles of biological aging

DOI 10.1093/pnasnexus/pgae499

Gladyshev, V. N., Anderson, B., Barlit, H., ..., Ying, K., Yunes, J., Zhang, B., & Zhavoronkov, A.

PNAS Nexus · 2024

Open paper

Summary

A survey of aging researchers found broad disagreement on what aging is, what causes it, when it begins and what constitutes rejuvenation. No surveyed question received a majority opinion, including the question of whether consensus is needed.
