Phosphorylation of PLIN3 by AMPK Promotes Dispersion of Lipid Droplets during Starvation

DOI 10.1007/s13238-018-0593-9

J Zhu, M Xu, Y Liu, L Zhuang, K Ying, F Liu, D Liu, W Ma, Z Songyang

Protein & Cell · 2019

Open paper

Summary

The study identifies PLIN3 as a physiological substrate of AMPK and suggests that phosphorylation may expose its C-terminus to promote lipid droplet dispersion.
