Longevity biotechnology: Bridging AI, biomarkers, geroscience and clinical applications for healthy longevity

DOI 10.18632/aging.206135

Lyu, YX., Fu, Q., Wilczok, D., Ying, K., King, A., ..., Bakula, D.

Aging · 2024

Open paper

Summary

This report from the 2023 Aging Research and Drug Discovery Meeting reviews developments in AI, aging biomarkers, geroscience and clinical interventions. It discusses how academic research and biotechnology efforts seek to translate aging biology into longer healthspan.
