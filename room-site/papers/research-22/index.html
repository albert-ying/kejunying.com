Genetic and Phenotypic Analysis of the Causal Relationship between Aging and COVID-19

DOI 10.1038/s43856-021-00033-z

K Ying, R Zhai, TV Pyrkov, AV Shindyapina, M Mariotti, PO Fedichev, X Shen, VN Gladyshev

Communications Medicine · 2021

Open paper

Summary

The genetic variation associated with longer lifespan is associated with reduced risk of COVID-19 infection and hospitalization, and some of the key cells and molecules involved in aging-related COVID-19 risk are identified.
