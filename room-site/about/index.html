ClockBase Agent

bioRxiv · 2024

MethylGPT: A Foundation Model for the DNA Methylome

K Ying, J Song, H Cui, Y Zhang,..., VN Gladyshev

MethylGPT learns the grammar of the human DNA methylome, how CpG sites are methylated together, without labels from 169,010 public methylation profiles. One model then reads any of four array platforms and carries what it learned into imputation, age, sex, tissue and aging-clock outputs.

One of the first foundation models for DNA methylation data, enabling transfer learning and improved predictions across diverse epigenetic tasks.

Analysis of centenarian genomes reveals depleted loss-of-function mutations in specific genes, identifying genetic variants that contribute to exceptional longevity.

About

Kejun Ying is an incoming assistant professor. He will start his own lab in the Bay Area in January 2027; the exact institution will be announced later. He is currently a postdoctoral fellow at Stanford University in Tony Wyss-Coray’s lab and at the University of Washington in David Baker’s lab, supported by an NIH/NIA F99K00 fellowship. He received the 2026 WAIC Yunfan Award.

His research focuses on AI for Science, with an emphasis on AI for Biology and AI for Aging. He develops foundation models and world models for biological data, and builds agentic systems. His work includes biological age modeling and aging intervention discovery, brain aging and neurodegeneration, systematic analyses of cancer and age-related diseases, and de novo protein design for aging interventions. His previous work includes a causal inference-based aging clock, ClockBase, and MethylGPT.
