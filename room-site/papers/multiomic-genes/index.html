Integrative epigenetics and transcriptomics identify aging genes in human blood

DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-67369-1

Moqri, M., Ying, K., Poganik, J. R., Herzog, C., …, Snyder, M. P., Gladyshev, V. N.

Nature Communications · 2026

Open paper

Summary

The study combines age-related DNA methylation changes with age-related gene expression changes in blood to identify multi-omic aging genes. These genes are enriched for adaptive immune functions. Compared with genes found from either data type alone, they replicate better across populations and are more strongly associated with aging-related outcomes.
