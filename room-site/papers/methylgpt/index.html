MethylGPT: A Foundation Model for the DNA Methylome

DOI 10.1101/2024.10.30.621013

K Ying, J Song, H Cui, Y Zhang, S Li, X Chen, …, VN Gladyshev

bioRxiv · 2024 · Preprint

Open paper

Summary

MethylGPT is a transformer foundation model trained on 154,063 human DNA methylation profiles from 5,281 datasets across tissues. It covers 49,156 CpG sites and predicts methylation values with a Pearson correlation of 0.93. Its downstream predictions stay stable with up to 70% of the data missing.

preprint · CC-BY-NC

Featured description

MethylGPT learns the grammar of the human DNA methylome, how CpG sites are methylated together, without labels from 169,010 public methylation profiles. One model then reads any of four array platforms and carries what it learned into imputation, age, sex, tissue and aging-clock outputs.
