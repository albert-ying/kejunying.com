An open competition for biomarkers of aging

DOI 10.1038/s43587-026-01139-6

Ying, K., Paulson, S., Reinhard, J., …, Gladyshev, V. N.

Nature Aging · 2026

Open paper

Summary

The Biomarkers of Aging Challenge was an open two-phase competition on blinded blood DNA-methylation and proteomic data from 500 Mass General Brigham Biobank participants. Models from the competing teams predicted chronological age more accurately than published clocks. A proteomic model predicted all-cause mortality better than GrimAge.
