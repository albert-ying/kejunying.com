Standardization of aging biomarkers and BoA challenge

Biolearn is an open-source library that standardizes public datasets and published clocks so that aging biomarkers can be compared on the same samples. The Biomarkers of Aging Challenge is an open competition in which teams predict chronological age, mortality and multi-morbidity from blinded biobank data.
