Open paper

ClockBase Agent uses AI agents to reanalyze millions of public human and mouse methylation and RNA-seq samples with more than 40 aging clocks. Across 43,602 intervention-control comparisons it identified more than 500 interventions associated with lower biological age. One candidate, ouabain, slowed frailty progression, reduced neuroinflammation and improved cardiac function in aged mice.
