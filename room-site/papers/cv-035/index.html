An unbiased comparison of 14 epigenetic clocks in relation to 174 incident disease outcomes

DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-66106-y

Mavrommatis, C., Belsky, D. W., Ying, K., Moqri, M., Campbell, A., Richmond, A., Gladyshev, V. N., Chandra, T., McCartney, D. L., & Marioni, R. E.

Nature Communications · 2025

Open paper

Summary

The study compared 14 epigenetic clocks against 174 incident diseases and mortality in 18,859 people over ten years. Second- and third-generation clocks outperformed first-generation clocks, with disease prediction particularly promising for respiratory and liver conditions.
