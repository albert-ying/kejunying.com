Causal Epigenetic Age Uncouples Damage and Adaptation

2023-08

10th Aging Research and Drug Discovery conference (ARDD2023)

Copenhagen, Denmark

Recording

Abstract

We performed epigenome-wide Mendelian Randomization using large-scale genetic data to identify CpG sites causally related to aging traits. Our findings suggest that neither existing epigenetic clocks nor age-related differential DNA methylation are enriched in putative causal CpG sites. We introduced DamAge and AdaptAge, measures of age-related damaging and adaptive changes respectively. DamAge acceleration correlates with adverse conditions, including mortality risk, while AdaptAge acceleration is linked to beneficial adaptations. Only DamAge is reversed upon cellular reprogramming. Our work provides a detailed map of CpG sites causally linked to lifespan and healthspan, enabling the development of causal biomarkers for aging and rejuvenation, and evaluation of longevity interventions.
