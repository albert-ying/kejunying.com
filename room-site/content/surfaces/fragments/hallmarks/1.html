The study combines more than 11,000 transcriptomes from more than 25 tissues of mouse, rat, macaque and human and uses them to build biomarkers of age and expected mortality. CDKN1A and LGALS3 are among the signatures of aging and mortality shared across species. The signatures fall into modules such as inflammation, interferon signaling and mitochondrial function, and interventions affect these modules differently.
