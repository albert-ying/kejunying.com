This review assesses how well omic biomarkers of aging predict outcomes in population studies and discusses why they are hard to compare and to generalize across populations. It recommends validation steps for clinical translation and for gerotherapeutic trials.

published

Current efforts to evaluate the predictive validity of omic biomarkers of aging in population studies are reviewed, challenges in comparability and generalizability are discussed, and recommendations to facilitate future validation of biomarkers of aging are provided
