Ageome estimates the epigenetic age of thousands of molecular pathways at once in mice and humans. Cell reprogramming rejuvenated cells overall yet accelerated the aging of some modules, and some module clocks predicted the onset of age-related disease better than existing clocks, especially for cancer.
