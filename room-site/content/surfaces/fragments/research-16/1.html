Molecular Cell · 2023

Open paper

Methyl-labeling experiments in C. elegans tracked inherited methylation in DNA, RNA, proteins and lipids. The study found that ribosomal RNA methyltransferases DIMT1 and BUD23 were required for the increased stress resistance and longevity seen in offspring of starved parents.
