High-dimensional Ageome Representations of Biological Aging across Functional Modules

DOI 10.1101/2024.09.17.613599

K Ying, A Tyshkovskiy, Q Chen, E Latorre-Crespo, B Zhang, H Liu, …, VN Gladyshev

bioRxiv · 2024 · Preprint

Open paper

Summary

Ageome estimates the epigenetic age of thousands of molecular pathways at once in mice and humans. Cell reprogramming rejuvenated cells overall yet accelerated the aging of some modules. Some module clocks predicted the onset of age-related disease better than existing clocks, especially for cancer.

preprint · CC-BY-NC

Featured description

Develops high-dimensional representations of biological aging that capture aging signatures across multiple functional modules including metabolomics, proteomics, and transcriptomics.
