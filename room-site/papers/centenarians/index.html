Depletion of Loss-of-function Germline Mutations in Centenarians Reveals Longevity Genes

DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-52967-2

K Ying, JP Castro, AV Shindyapina, A Tyshkovskiy, M Moqri, LJE Goeminne, …, VN Gladyshev

Nature Communications · 2024

Open paper

Summary

Whole-exome sequencing of Ashkenazi Jewish cohorts showed that centenarians carry an 11 to 22% lower burden of rare loss-of-function mutations than controls, and their offspring show a similar pattern. Gene-level tests with Mendelian randomization and multi-omic data identified RGP1, PCNX2 and ANO9 as longevity genes.

published · CC BY-NC-ND

Featured description

Analysis of centenarian genomes reveals depleted loss-of-function mutations in specific genes, identifying genetic variants that contribute to exceptional longevity.
