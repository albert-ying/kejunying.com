A world model simulates the latent dynamics of human health

Wang, Z., Zhang, O., Wang, J., Jiang, Y., Ma, Z. C., …, Gladyshev, V. N., Wyss-Coray, T., Ying, K. · medRxiv · 2026

HealthFlux is a world model built from 502,166 UK Biobank participants. It treats a person's health as one hidden state that changes between visits and is updated by each new blood test, diagnosis, molecular profile or brain and body MRI. In held-out participants it predicts 1,256 diseases and death over twenty years, and it was tested in four independent US cohorts.

Autonomous AI Agents Discover Aging Interventions from Millions of Molecular Profiles

K Ying, A Tyshkovskiy, A Moldakozhayev, H Wang, …, L Cong, A Regev, J Leskovec, T Wyss-Coray, VN Gladyshev · bioRxiv · 2025

ClockBase Agent uses AI agents to reanalyze millions of public human and mouse methylation and RNA-seq samples with more than 40 aging clocks. Across 43,602 intervention-control comparisons it found more than 500 interventions associated with lower biological age. One candidate, ouabain, slowed frailty progression and reduced neuroinflammation in aged mice.

A Unified Framework for Systematic Curation and Evaluation of Aging Biomarkers

K Ying, S Paulson, A Eames, A Tyshkovskiy, …, VN Gladyshev · Nature Aging · 2025

Biolearn is an open-source library that standardizes public datasets and published clocks so that aging biomarkers can be compared on the same samples. It evaluates 39 aging biomarkers on standardized data from more than 20,000 people.
