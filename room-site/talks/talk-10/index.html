Event description

The lack of reliable biomarkers of aging is one of the biggest challenges and bottlenecks hindering the efforts of the longevity community to develop safe and effective gerotherapeutics. The Biomarkers of Aging Challenge is a recently launched, open-science initiative by the Biomarkers of Aging Consortium stimulating innovation and collaboration in the development of next-generation biomarkers that are highly predictive of chronological age, mortality, and multi-morbidity to support the translation of gerotherapeutics.
