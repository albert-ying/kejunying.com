Epigenome-wide Mendelian randomization identifies CpG sites potentially causal for aging-related traits. From them we built DamAge and AdaptAge, clocks that measure damaging and adaptive changes separately. Applied to public intervention datasets through ClockBase, such clocks can screen for treatments that lower biological age.
